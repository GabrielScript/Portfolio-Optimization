# Attempt 55: the Composite with 4 names from the top-10 returns ("Top10"), pre-registration

Date: 2026-11-04. Requested by the user on 2026-11-03 ("3 or 4 of the portfolio's stocks among the 10 best returns,
but not the ones that already peaked, like M1RN34, MUTC34 and JSLG3"); the user approved testing it on 2026-11-04.
This file is committed before any code of the arm and before any result. The reading happens once.

## 1. Question

Does forcing up to 4 of the 15 Composite names to come from the 10 best 12-month returns, leaving out the ones
already falling from their recent peak, beat the Composite itself (attempt 51, the app's real-money plan since
DECISOES 242), out of sample and on the survivorship-free lake?

## 2. Data and engine (frozen, identical to attempt 51)

- Prices, point-in-time universe (delisted names included), sectors, monthly panel and dividends:
  `docs/dados/2026-10-22` (hashes checked by the loaders); last session 16/10/2026.
- CVM statements of the Composite's value and growth scores: the same folder attempt 51 read.
- Engine: `walk_forward_dinamico`, training window 1,260 sessions, rebalance every 63 sessions, R$ 10,000,
  10 bps a side, the fixed risk rules (`checar_carteira`, a violation keeps the current portfolio).
- Phases: 0, 21 and 42 sessions cut from the start (`FASES`), as every reading since attempt 41.

## 3. The arm (one configuration, no variants)

At each decision (the training window's last session), on the names the engine leaves eligible:

1. The Composite's score of the month (attempt 51's `_value_score(mensal, com_crescimento=True)`), unchanged.
2. **Return window:** the total return of the last 252 sessions of the training window (12 months up to the
   decision, from the engine's daily total-return training data; no skipped month).
3. **Top 10:** the 10 names with the highest 252-session return among those with a Composite score.
4. **"Already peaked":** a top-10 name is left out when its total-return index at the decision is 15% or more below
   its highest level of the last 63 sessions (the window's starting level included).
5. **4 names:** the top-10 names left, in the order of the Composite score, enter first, up to 4, under the
   Composite's own skips (no sector, a second class of a chosen issuer, a sector that already has 4).
6. The other places, up to 15, are filled in the Composite's order with the same skips. Equal weights.

Fewer than 4 qualifying names means fewer forced names; no qualifying name gives the Composite's portfolio.
"3 or 4" was settled at 4 before the test, so the count is one trial, not two.

## 4. Gate (all must hold in all 3 phases)

- P[> Composite] >= 0.95: PSR of the daily return difference against the Composite (`psr_da_diferenca`).
- DSR >= 0.95: the deflated Sharpe of the information ratio against BOVA11, N from `docs/tentativas.csv` with this
  row added.

Reported, not gating: P[> Conservadora], P[> BOVA11], CAGR, maximum drawdown, turnover, refusals, the year table and
how many forced names each rebalance had.

## 5. Decision

- Gate passed: the arm becomes a candidate; it needs a forward paper log of its own before it can replace the
  Composite in the plan. Nothing changes in the app the day it passes.
- Gate failed: the rule is closed; the plan stays the Composite. Not retried with other windows, thresholds or
  counts (that would be a new, separately counted trial).

## 6. Limits

The peak rule uses only prices up to the decision; M1RN34, MUTC34 and JSLG3 inspired it but were not looked up to
tune it. BDRs are outside the frozen stock universe, as for every Composite reading. After costs, before IR.
